# Judge instructions

Open https://spaceproof.onrender.com/. If the page is slow, the free web service may be waking up. Wait for the catalog, then refresh once if it stays on the loading line.

## What to do

1. Read the first sentence. SpaceProof checks a confirmed area before PayPal can authorize a price.
2. Choose **Sample room**, or upload your own JPEG or PNG under 8 MB. The picture suggests a rectangle. It does not supply feet or inches.
3. Move or resize the rectangle if you disagree with the dashed AI suggestion. A solid outline means you edited it.
4. Confirm the feet and inches. The demo preset is a 4 ft 11.06 in by 3 ft 11.24 in opening, with 7.87 in of clearance and a $180 budget. Those are typed values, not a measurement of the drawing.
5. Select **Nook Desk**. A current `FIT` can open PayPal Sandbox after you approve the catalog price of $129.00.
6. Select **Studio Desk** in the same room, or switch to the 3 ft 3.37 in preset. Checkout should say **Not eligible**.
7. Do not expect a completed charge until a sandbox buyer approves the PayPal window and the server verifies the capture.

## What not to expect

- A measurement taken from the photograph.
- A guarantee about doorways, delivery, or assembly.
- Live PayPal money. The app refuses a non-sandbox environment.
- A published video. The recording script is `docs/submission/demo-script.md`.

## If something fails

- Vision or Nebius down: draw the area yourself. Fit and PayPal do not require the model.
- Catalog unavailable: the database is not reachable. That is a hosting problem, not a fit result.
- PayPal button missing: the server did not store a current `FIT`, or the sandbox client id is not configured. The page should say why.

Sandbox buyer credentials stay with the owner. They are not in the repository.
